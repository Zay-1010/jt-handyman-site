Rental Minimum Standards 2027

Know exactly what's changing

These upgrades become mandatory for rentals, most triggered the moment a lease is signed or renewed. We handle every item below and keep each property compliance ready.

From 1 Mar 2027

Ceiling insulation

Insulation to R5.0 required where none currently exists. If any is already present, no upgrade is needed.

Triggered at lease changes
From 1 Mar 2027

4 star showerheads

All showers must have 4 star WELS rated showerheads, up from the current 3 star minimum.

Triggered at lease changes
From 1 Jul 2027

Draught proofing

External doors, windows and unsealed wall vents must be weather sealed.

Triggered at lease changes
New lease · from 1 Mar 2027

Efficient cooling

A fixed efficient electric cooling system, 3 star or above, required in the main living area.

Triggered on new lease
On appliance failure

Heating & hot water

A failed heater must be replaced with efficient electric (reverse cycle); failed hot water with a heat pump or solar system.

Triggered on failure
By 1 Jul 2030

All rental homes

Must be energy efficient, with cooling required in the main living area, across every rental property.

Portfolio wide deadline
