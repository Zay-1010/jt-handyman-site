Built fast, built right

From one pair of hands to a 24-strong team — in under two years

Jabez started JT on his own with a simple frustration: trades that didn't turn up, didn't communicate, and left jobs half-finished. So he built the opposite. Instead of scaling on promises, he scaled on systems — the quoting, the scheduling, the office liaising with tenants and owners, the photos at the end. Get those right and the work looks after itself.

That focus on speed and follow-through is the whole reason JT grew the way it did. Property managers, real estate agents and homeowners came back because the job got done, on time, to a high standard — and they didn't have to chase anyone to make it happen.
