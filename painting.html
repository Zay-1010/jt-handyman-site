Common questions

Painting & plastering, answered

Do you repair walls before painting?

Yes — cracks, holes and damaged plaster are prepared and repaired before any paint goes on, not covered over.

Can you paint just one room or a small area?

Yes — from a single feature wall through to a full property, jobs are scoped and estimated to fit what you actually need.

Do you paint doors, skirting and other trim?

Yes — trim, doors and skirting are part of the job, finished to match the rest of the room.

Can you help choose the right paint or finish?

Yes — we can help you choose a colour and finish that suits the room and holds up to everyday wear.

Can painting and plaster repairs be done as one job?

Yes — since it's the same team, plaster repairs and painting can be estimated and completed together as one job.
