Why paint jobs don't last

The finish is only as good as the prep underneath it

A coat of paint rolled straight over old cracks, dust or a chalky surface looks fine for a few weeks, then it peels, flashes or shows every flaw underneath. JT's painters prep properly first — filling, sanding, sealing — so the finish actually holds and looks clean under any light, not just on handover day.

  • Full surface prep before a single coat goes on
  • Interior and exterior, feature walls to full repaints
  • Clean lines, clean site, no overspray on your furniture

Common questions

Painting & plastering, answered

Do you handle prep, or just the paint?

Prep is included — filling, sanding, masking and priming are part of the job, not an optional extra you have to ask for.

Can you match my existing wall colour?

In most cases yes, from an existing tin, swatch, or by colour-matching on site before we start.

Will you protect my furniture and floors?

Yes — proper masking and drop sheets are standard on every job, not something we skip to save time.
