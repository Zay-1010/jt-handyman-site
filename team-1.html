Our ethos

We treat your home like it's our own.

When you let someone into your home, you're trusting them with more than a repair. We get that. So we take the little things seriously: turning up on time, being tidy and respectful, explaining what we're doing, and leaving a place better than we found it. What we believe

Look after each other. Look after you.

The way a team treats each other is the way they'll treat your home. So we started with that. These are the things we care about most, and they're the same reasons our customers keep asking for us back.

We genuinely care

We hire good people first, because you can teach a skill but you can't teach someone to care. The person on your job wants it done right just as much as you do, and it shows in the details.

We're honest with you

No surprises, no runaround. We tell you what a job really needs, what it'll cost and how long it'll take, and if something changes you'll hear it from us first. Trust is the whole point.

We keep you in the loop

Our office stays in touch with you, and with tenants and owners where it matters, right through the job. You're never left wondering what's happening or when someone's coming.

We back each other

Nobody on the team is left on their own, and that carries onto your job. There's always more than one set of hands and eyes on the work, and a crew that helps each other get it right.

We show up

When we say we'll be there, we're there. It sounds simple, but it's the thing people are most relieved by, and we never treat it as optional. Your time matters as much as ours.

We take pride in it

The crew takes real pride in the finish, done properly the first time and backed by our workmanship guarantee. The photos at the end aren't just for you, they're what the team's proud of too.

Why we do it this way

It started with one simple belief.

Jabez started JT on his own at 22, tired of seeing people let down by trades who didn't turn up, didn't call back and didn't care. He believed people deserved better, and that if you treat customers, and the team, with genuine respect, the rest looks after itself.

In just under two years that belief turned one pair of hands into a team of more than twenty. Not because of clever marketing, but because looking after people works. Look after your crew and they look after your customers, and your customers tell their friends. That's the whole story of JT, and it's still the only thing we're really trying to get right.

  • Built on genuine care, not shortcuts
  • Grew because people kept recommending us
  • Trusted by homeowners, investors and property managers
