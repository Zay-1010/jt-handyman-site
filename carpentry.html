Why the small jobs get skipped

Carpentry is where most trades quietly say no

A dropped door, a rotten frame, a verandah post that's given way — too small for a builder, too specific for a handyman with no carpentry background. JT was built on carpentry, so these are exactly the jobs our in-house team picks up, assesses properly and fixes so they stay fixed.

  • Doors, frames, hardware & locks
  • Built-ins, shelving, panelling & trim
  • Verandahs, handrails & period restoration
What we fix

Carpentry, door to deck

Doors that stick or won't latch

Dropped hinges, swollen frames, worn strike plates — re-hung, planed or re-fitted so the door closes and locks properly again.

Rotten or damaged frames

Timber frames replaced or repaired section by section, matched to the existing profile rather than a full rip-out.

Built-ins & shelving

Floating shelves, VJ panelling, storage and feature walls — built to fit the room, not off-the-shelf.

Handrails & balustrades

Loose, unsafe or non-compliant handrails re-fixed or replaced — stainless, timber or painted finishes.

Verandahs & decking

Posts, brackets, decorative timber and structural repairs for verandahs and decks that have started to sag or split.

Period & heritage carpentry

Arches, cornice-adjacent timberwork and older detailing restored to match, not replaced with something generic.

One team, start to finish

Carpentry today, paint or plaster tomorrow — same crew

New timber almost always needs paint or plaster work around it. Because JT's carpenters, painters and plasterers are the same in-house team, your carpentry job can roll straight into a paint match or wall patch without a second booking or a second quote.

Common questions

Carpentry, answered

Can you match existing timber and trim?

In most cases, yes — we match profile, timber type and finish as closely as possible rather than defaulting to a generic replacement.

Do you handle both repair and full replacement?

Both. We'll always tell you honestly which one your job actually needs rather than upselling a full replacement when a repair will do.

Can the same visit cover paint or plaster too?

Yes — since it's the same in-house team, we can quote carpentry alongside any paint or plaster work the job needs in one visit.
