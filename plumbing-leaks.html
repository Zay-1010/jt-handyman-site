Why the drip keeps coming back

A tightened tap only buys a few weeks

A dripping tap that's just tightened up will usually start again, because the actual wear (a washer, a valve, a seal) hasn't been replaced. JT checks what's actually failed before touching it, so the fix holds instead of dripping again by next month.

  • Washers, valves and seals replaced, not just tightened
  • Slow drains checked for the actual blockage, not just plunged
  • Licensed plumbing brought in exactly where the job requires it
