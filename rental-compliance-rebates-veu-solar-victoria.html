Rebates that cut your 2027 compliance cost: VEU and Solar Victoria explained

The Victorian Energy Upgrades program and Solar for Rentals rebate can offset most of the cost of meeting the new rental minimum energy efficiency standards, and JT Compliance applies both across your whole rent roll.

Updated 14 July 2026 · 6 min read · By JT Compliance

In short: Victorian landlords can cut the cost of the 2027 rental minimum standards using two programs: Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU), which discounts heating, cooling, hot water, draught sealing, showerheads and ceiling insulation (insulation discount up to $1,482 from 1 Oct 2026, expected around 1 Jan 2027 for rentals), and Solar for Rentals, which pays a rebate up to $1,400 plus a matching interest-free loan up to $1,400 (total up to $2,800 off a solar system).

Key facts

  • VEU discounts are available now for heating, cooling, hot water, draught and weather sealing, and showerheads across Victorian rentals.
  • The VEU ceiling insulation discount pays up to $1,482 (minimum $200 customer contribution) from 1 Oct 2026 for general homes, expected around 1 Jan 2027 for rentals.
  • Solar for Rentals (Solar Victoria) pays a rebate up to $1,400 plus a matching interest-free loan up to $1,400, for a total of up to $2,800 off a solar system.
  • The Solar for Rentals renter household income cap drops from $210,000 to $150,000 from 1 Jul 2026; the property must be valued under $3 million.
  • A maximum of 2 rental solar rebates apply per financial year, and the property address must not have been previously rebated.
  • Solar PV is an incentive only, not a minimum standard; the new energy efficiency standards start from 1 Mar 2027.

Victorian landlords can offset most of the cost of the new rental minimum energy efficiency standards using two government programs. The Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) scheme discounts heating, cooling, hot water systems, draught and weather sealing, showerheads and ceiling insulation. Solar for Rentals, run by Solar Victoria, pays a rebate of up to $1,400 plus a matching interest-free loan of up to $1,400, so a solar system can cost up to $2,800 less. Used together and timed correctly, these programs turn a large one-off compliance bill into a manageable, staged spend. JT Compliance applies both across an entire rent roll, so the rebate is baked into the quote rather than left for the owner to chase.

What government rebates exist for Victorian rental compliance?

There are two programs that matter for compliance costs. The first is VEU, which is a discount at the point of installation delivered through accredited providers. The second is Solar for Rentals, a Solar Victoria rebate plus loan that reduces the cost of a rooftop solar system. VEU covers the appliances and works that the new standards actually require. Solar for Rentals covers rooftop solar, which is not a minimum standard but is a genuine incentive that reduces a landlord's outgoings and improves the property.

The new standards themselves come from the Residential Tenancies Amendment (Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards) Regulations 2025, finalised and staged from 1 Mar 2027. Most upgrades are triggered by a new lease, a conversion to a periodic agreement, or an appliance failing at end of life. The one hard portfolio deadline is efficient cooling in the main living area of every home by 1 Jul 2030.

Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU): what it discounts and when

The VEU program, administered through Energy Victoria, offers discounts now on the exact items the standards call for: efficient heating, efficient cooling, hot water systems, draught and weather sealing, and showerheads. That means a landlord upgrading ahead of the 1 Mar 2027 lease trigger can claim the discount today rather than waiting.

The one item with a future start date is ceiling insulation. The VEU ceiling insulation discount opens for general homes from 1 Oct 2026, worth up to $1,482 with a minimum $200 customer contribution. For rental properties specifically, the insulation discount is expected to apply from around 1 Jan 2027. Because the ceiling insulation standard itself starts on 1 Mar 2027, that timing lines up well: a landlord who needs to install R5.0 insulation where none exists can claim the VEU discount just before the standard bites.

Solar for Rentals: up to $2,800 off a solar system

Solar for Rentals is a separate program from Solar Victoria. It pays a rebate of up to $1,400 toward a rooftop solar system, and a landlord can add a matching interest-free loan of up to $1,400 repaid over four years. Combined, that is up to $2,800 off the cost of solar. Solar PV is not a minimum standard, so no landlord is required to install it, but for owners across Melbourne and regional Victoria who want a stronger asset and a more attractive listing, the numbers are worth running.

The eligibility rules are specific. From 1 Jul 2026 the renter household income cap drops from $210,000 to $150,000, so the household living in the property must earn under $150,000 to qualify. The property must be valued at under $3 million, the address must not have been previously rebated, and a maximum of 2 rental rebates apply per financial year.

How do VEU and Solar for Rentals work together?

They are complementary, not competing. VEU discounts the compliance items the standards require, so it directly reduces the cost of meeting the law. Solar for Rentals discounts an optional upgrade that lowers the tenant's power bills and lifts the property's appeal. A landlord in Reservoir replacing a failed gas heater with an efficient electric unit claims VEU on the heater. The same landlord can, in the same visit, look at whether the roof and the tenant's income qualify for Solar for Rentals. Sequencing the works so both programs are captured is where the savings add up.

When should landlords act to capture the rebates?

The advertising reform is already in force. Since 25 Nov 2025, a rental property must meet the existing minimum standards before it is advertised or when an offer to let is made, not just at handover. That changes the timing for any property between tenancies now.

For the new energy efficiency standards, the trigger points are staggered from 1 Mar 2027, with draught proofing from 1 Jul 2027 and the cooling deadline for all homes on 1 Jul 2030. Acting before a lease turns over lets a landlord claim VEU discounts that are already open, and plan insulation around the 1 Oct 2026 general start (roughly 1 Jan 2027 for rentals). Leaving every property to its lease trigger at once creates a crunch and forgoes the chance to sequence works efficiently.

How JT Compliance applies the rebates across a portfolio

JT Compliance works as one Melbourne partner for a whole rent roll rather than property by property. The process is consistent. First, JT audits every property against the 14 existing minimum standards, in force since 29 Mar 2021, and the incoming energy efficiency standards. Second, JT quotes the works with the VEU discount and any Solar for Rentals rebate already applied, so the number a property manager sees is the net cost, not a gross figure to reconcile later. Third, JT completes the works using accredited installers so the discounts are valid. Fourth, JT hands over documented reports for each property, which matters because renters enforce these standards through VCAT and Consumer Affairs Victoria is the regulator, with the 2025 to 2026 penalty unit set at $203.51.
