Why bathrooms leak again

Most leaks get re-sealed, not re-waterproofed

A bead of silicone around a shower screen looks like a fix, but if the membrane underneath has failed, water's still getting into the wall or the floor below. JT checks whether it's a surface seal issue or a waterproofing failure before doing anything, so the repair actually stops the water, not just the visible drip.

  • Surface seal failures treated differently from membrane failures
  • Wall and floor cavities checked, not just the visible tile line
  • Grout and silicone replaced to spec, not just topped up
