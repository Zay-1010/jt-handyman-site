Common questions

Outdoor construction, answered

Can you repair an existing deck or fence, or only build new?

Both — from a full new build through to repairing or restoring what's already there, we'll assess what your job actually needs.

Do you handle council permits or approvals?

We'll let you know upfront if your job needs council approval and can guide you through what's required.

Can you match new fencing or decking to what's already there?

Where possible, yes — we'll try to match existing materials and style so new work blends with the rest of the property.
