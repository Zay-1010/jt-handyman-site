Get Your Home Ready for Summer Entertaining

8 things to check, fix or freshen up before Christmas.

The checklist

8 things to check, fix or freshen up

1

Decks

Soft, grey or splitting boards? Sand, re-oil or replace tired timbers so it's safe and looking its best for entertaining.

2

Pergolas and verandas

Check for peeling paint, worn timbers and loose fixings. A repaint or a few new timbers can make it feel brand new.

3

Doors, windows and flyscreens

Check that sliding doors run smoothly, seals close tightly and screens have no tears, to keep the heat and bugs out.

4

Exterior painting and touch-ups

Faded fences, trims and outdoor walls show up when guests arrive. A fresh coat makes the whole space feel new.

5

Pressure washing

Paths, driveways, decks and outdoor areas come up like new with a proper clean before the festive season.

6

Fences and gates

Look for leaning posts, loose palings and sagging gates before summer winds and visitors put them to the test.

7

Garden maintenance

Tidy garden beds, trim overgrown areas and clear outdoor spaces so they're ready to enjoy.

8

Outdoor taps and smoke alarms

Fix dripping taps before the long, hot days, and test every smoke alarm. It takes five minutes.
