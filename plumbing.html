Why leaks come back

A tightened fitting isn't always the fix

A drip that keeps returning, or a drain that's slow again a month later, usually means the real cause was never addressed — just the symptom. JT traces the actual source before calling a job done, so the fix holds instead of you booking the same repair twice.

  • Leaks traced to the real source, not just patched
  • Everyday faults sorted alongside the rest of your job
  • Licensed plumbing brought in wherever the work requires it
What we fix

Plumbing, tap to drain

Dripping taps

Washers, cartridges and worn fittings replaced so the drip actually stops, not just slows down.

Slow or blocked drains

Kitchen, bathroom and laundry drains cleared and checked for the underlying cause of repeat blockages.

Leaking traps & pipes

Under-sink leaks, joint failures and everyday pipe faults found and properly resealed or replaced.

Toilet repairs

Running cisterns, weak flushes and leaks at the base repaired or replaced.

Hot water issues

No hot water, inconsistent temperature or a unit reaching the end of its life — assessed and sorted.

Tapware & fixtures

New taps, mixers and fixtures supplied and fitted, matched to your kitchen or bathroom.

One team, start to finish

Plumbing faults rarely show up on their own — a leaking tap sits next to tiling, a slow drain sits under cabinetry. Because JT's plumbers work alongside our tilers and carpenters, the whole job is quoted and scheduled together instead of you coordinating separate trades.
