Why now

Melbourne's cold months find every weak window

A window that was fine in summer suddenly whistles, sticks or fogs up once the temperature drops. Swollen timber, perished seals and old putty all show up hardest in Melbourne's cooler months — and a drafty window is quietly costing you on heating every single day it's left as-is. JT's team restores frames rather than defaulting straight to a costly full replacement.

  • Frame restoration for period & older homes
  • Re-sealing to cut draughts and heating loss
  • Cracked or fogged glass replacement
What we fix

Windows, frame to glass

Deteriorated frames

Rotten, swollen or peeling timber frames restored section by section — keeping the original character instead of ripping it all out.

Sticking or jammed windows

Cracked or fogged glass

Single panes and failed double-glazed units replaced, re-glazed and puttied to a clean, weatherproof finish.

Flyscreens

Common questions

Windows & glazing, answered

Do I need to replace the whole window, or can it be restored?

Most timber frames can be restored rather than replaced, especially on older and period homes — we'll always tell you honestly which one your window actually needs.

Can you match the original frame style?

Yes — restoration is exactly for keeping the original profile and character, rather than swapping in a generic modern frame.

Will fixing the draught actually make a difference?

Yes — perished seals and gaps around window frames are one of the most common sources of heat loss in older Melbourne homes through winter.
