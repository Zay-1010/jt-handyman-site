What's underneath matters

A tile job is only as good as what's underneath it

A quick re-grout over a failed waterproofing membrane looks fine for a few months, then the leak comes back through the ceiling below. JT checks the waterproofing and substrate before anything gets tiled over, so the fix holds for years, not months.

Common questions

Bathrooms & tiling, answered

Is my leak the tiling or the waterproofing?

Often it's the membrane underneath, not the tile itself. We check both before quoting so you're not paying to fix the wrong thing.

Can you match my existing tiles?

Where possible yes — for repairs we'll try to source a close match; for larger jobs we can help select a full new tile range.

Do you handle full renovations, not just repairs?

Yes — from a single re-grout through to a complete bathroom refit, coordinated by one point of contact.
